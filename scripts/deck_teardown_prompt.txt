[ABSOLUTE RULE — EVIDENCE OR NOTHING]
Every object you emit MUST carry an "evidence" field containing the VERBATIM text from the
deck that the claim came from, copied character for character. If you cannot copy the

parser, so inventing, rounding, converting units, or paraphrasing a number will simply
delete your row. Copy the number exactly as printed in the deck.

Never infer, never estimate, never carry a figure over from your own knowledge of the
company. Omission is always correct when the deck is silent. Empty arrays are expected and
are a valid answer.

[CATEGORY GUIDANCE — metrics]
Capture the OPERATING engine, not the financial summary. Revenue, EBITDA and PAT are taken
from the audited filing elsewhere and must NOT be emitted here.
  capacity     installed / commissioned / planned capacity, with its period
  utilisation  capacity utilisation %, plant load factor, occupancy
  orderbook    order book, pipeline, backlog, book-to-bill
  volume       tonnes, units, subscribers, rooms, MW — anything counted not valued
  realisation  price per unit, ARPU, spread per tonne, yield
  segment_mix  revenue or volume share by product / business segment
  geo_mix      revenue or volume share by geography
Do NOT emit sustainability, ESG, diversity or emissions targets. They are not operating
metrics and they crowd out the ones that matter.

[DIFF GUIDANCE]
Only fill "diff" if a [PRIOR_DECK] block is supplied below. With no prior deck, return an
empty diff array — do not guess what changed.
  kpi_dropped        a metric present in [PRIOR_DECK] that this deck does not show at all.
                     This is the highest-value finding in the whole task: companies rarely
                     announce bad news, they stop mentioning it.
  target_moved       a date or number that shifted outward (FY27 -> FY28, 8 MT -> 7 MT).
  definition_changed same label, different basis (order book now including LOIs).
  new_emphasis       given materially more prominence than in the prior deck.
  de_emphasised      moved to the annexure, shrunk, or reduced to a footnote.

[FLAG GUIDANCE]

of. If axis values are not legible in the extracted text, emit nothing for that chart.
  absolute_to_percent  a series shown in absolute terms last period now shown only as a

  non_gaap_introduced  an adjusted/normalised/pre-exceptional metric appearing for the

  caveat_in_footnote   a material qualification placed in small print under a headline claim.
